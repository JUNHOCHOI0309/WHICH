# AI Image Moderation Policy Change Draft v1

Status: internal draft; legal review required before publication  
Task: WHICH-97

This document prepares future changes for a user-image pilot. It does not mean that WHICH currently
sends user images to an external AI provider or that general Member image upload is open.

## Privacy Policy draft

When the image-moderation feature is explicitly activated, WHICH first removes metadata, reduces
the image size, re-encodes it, and applies local privacy screening. Only the minimized review image
and redacted context needed for safety classification may be transferred to a named external
processor. WHICH does not transfer the uploader's raw IP, device ID, OAuth identifier, email,
Member/Guest identifier, or Vote choice for this purpose.

Before publication, the table must name each active processor and state its purpose, transferred
items, processing country/region, transfer timing and method, retention/deletion period, refusal
consequences where applicable, and contact/rights path. Legal review must confirm the correct Korean
international-transfer basis and wording.

WHICH does not use image moderation for face recognition, identity matching, biometric templates,
or demographic/sensitive-attribute inference. Face presence may only route a case to human review.

## Community Policy draft

- Uploaded images remain private until the applicable review path is complete.
- Automated signals are recommendations and do not determine rights, identity, defamation,
  copyright ownership, minor status, permanent deletion, or long account restrictions.
- A provider failure does not approve an image. The image remains private pending or the user may
  continue with a text-only/approved-library Issue.
- Users must not upload personal information without authority, sexual exploitation material,
  unlawful content, impersonation, harassment, or content for which they lack the necessary rights.
- WHICH may reversibly quarantine an already published image when a credible urgent safety/privacy
  signal arrives, with operator review and notice.

Legal review must align these clauses with the Terms, uploader consent/rights assertion, sanction
policy, and minors/real-person policy.

## Appeal and rights guide draft

- A notice identifies the affected image, policy category, action, date, and appeal eligibility
  without exposing sensitive detection details or another reporter's identity.
- An appeal can include a short explanation and necessary evidence. Collect only what is needed.
- Rights requests for privacy, defamation, or copyright use the dedicated Rights Desk, not the
  ordinary report score.
- A human makes the final appeal/rights decision and can fully restore visibility, capability, and
  related reversible sanctions.
- Appeal evidence is retained for 180 days after resolution. The proposed rights-evidence period is
  1,095 days after resolution and requires legal approval. Active legal hold overrides ordinary
  deletion until authorized release.

Legal review must confirm user-notice exceptions, statutory response periods, evidence retention,
identity verification, and jurisdiction/contact information.
